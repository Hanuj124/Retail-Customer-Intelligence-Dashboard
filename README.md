# Retail Customer Intelligence & Revenue Optimization Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to identify revenue drivers, customer loyalty patterns, seasonal trends, discount dependency, and purchasing preferences across different customer segments.

The objective of the analysis is to help retail businesses make data-driven decisions to improve customer retention, optimize marketing strategies, and increase overall business performance.

The project combines Python, SQL, and Tableau to simulate a real-world business analytics workflow used in modern retail organizations.

---

# Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand customer purchasing behavior across demographics, product categories, shipping methods, subscriptions, and discounts.

The management team aims to answer key business questions such as:

- Which customer groups generate the highest loyalty?
- Do subscription members spend more?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which products are overly dependent on discounts?
- How do seasonal trends impact sales performance?
- Which customer segments should be targeted for retention strategies?

# Project Workflow

## 1. Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering (Python)

Performed data cleaning and preprocessing using Python libraries such as Pandas and NumPy.

### Tasks Performed:
- Handled missing and inconsistent values
- Standardized categorical variables
- Created derived columns such as:
  - Age Group
  - Purchase Frequency Days
  - Loyalty Segmentation
- Prepared structured analytical dataset for SQL and Tableau analysis

### Tools Used:
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 2. Business Analysis Using SQL

Used MySQL to solve business-driven analytical questions related to customer behavior, loyalty, revenue contribution, discount dependency, and purchasing trends.

### Key Business Questions Solved:
- Which customer groups show the strongest loyalty?
- Does subscription membership improve customer retention?
- Which products and categories drive the highest revenue?
- Which products are highly dependent on discounts?
- Do highly rated products generate stronger loyalty?
- Which demographics respond best to discounts?
- Which payment methods are associated with high-value customers?
- Which seasons drive the highest customer engagement?

### SQL Concepts Used:
- Aggregations
- CASE WHEN
- CTEs
- Window Functions
- Joins
- Subqueries
- Ranking Functions
- Grouping & Segmentation

---

## 3. Interactive Dashboard Development (Tableau)

Built an interactive business intelligence dashboard in Tableau to visualize customer behavior, loyalty patterns, category performance, seasonal sales trends, and operational insights.

### Dashboard Features:
- KPI Tracking
- Revenue Analysis
- Customer Loyalty Segmentation
- Seasonal Revenue Trends
- Shipping & Payment Insights
- Customer Demographic Analysis
- Interactive Filters

### Dashboard KPIs:
- Total Revenue
- Average Purchase Amount

# Key Business Insights

- Loyal customers contribute the largest share of total revenue.
- Clothing category generates the highest revenue contribution.
- Subscription customers demonstrate stronger repeat purchase behavior.
- Certain products rely heavily on discounts for sales generation.
- Seasonal demand patterns significantly influence purchasing behavior.
- Customer demographics show different category preferences and spending patterns.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Focus retention campaigns on loyal customer segments.
- Reduce excessive dependency on discounts for low-performing products.
- Increase inventory allocation for high-performing seasonal categories.
- Implement demographic-based personalized marketing strategies.
- Encourage subscription adoption to improve customer retention.
